Seek privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Seek is a personal study library for sacred texts, made by Bryan Calle. This page explains what Seek does with your information. The short version: your study stays on your device. We do not collect it, and we cannot see it.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Seek lives only on your iPhone or iPad:

  • your highlights, underlines, circles and handwriting
  • your notes, notebooks and pages, including pages written beside scripture
  • your footnotes and the connections you draw between passages
  • scans and photos you add to your library
  • your reading place and your settings, including which hand you write with and how Compare is laid out
  • your reading history (the chapters you read for a while and the texts you compared), which powers "Continue reading"

Seek has no account, no sign-in and no server. Reading and studying need no internet connection.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your information.
  • We do not track you, and we do not use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not read your notes, marks, scans or reading history. Nothing in Seek sends them to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not ask about your faith or background.

Seek includes one third-party code library, GRDB, which runs on your device to read the scripture database. It does not send anything anywhere.

The camera and photos

If you choose to scan a document, Seek asks for camera access. The pages you scan are saved on your device, in your Seek library. They are not uploaded. Seek can also read the words on a scan so you can copy and search them; this happens on your device and the text is kept with the scan. You can turn camera access off at any time in the Settings app.

Reading history

Reading history is kept only on your device and is never used for analytics. It is left out of backups unless you turn on "Include Reading History in Backups". You can pause it with "Keep Reading History" in Settings, and erase it from the Backup & Restore screen. Turning it off stops new entries; it does not erase what is already saved.

Backups, exports and sharing: your choice

Seek can save a backup of your study, export it as Markdown, turn a notebook page into a PDF handout, and share a verse as a picture or as text. These happen only when you ask. The file or picture goes where you send it (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Messages or Mail), and from then on it is governed by that app or service's own policy, not this one. Your device's own backups (such as an iCloud or computer backup made by iOS) may include Seek's data; that is controlled in your device's settings.

Seek also keeps automatic backup copies, unless you turn this off in Backup & Restore. When you leave the app after making changes, it saves a copy of your study (at most once an hour, one file per day, the last 7 days). The copies are kept in Seek's own folder on your device, which you can see in the Files app, or in a folder you choose, such as one in iCloud Drive. If you choose an iCloud Drive folder, Apple stores those files under your iCloud account and its terms. Seek never sends the copies to us.

Links

If you tap a link in Seek, such as a website saved on a footnote, your device opens it in the browser or app that handles it. That site has its own privacy policy. Seek does not follow links for you.

Support and donations

Seek shows a support email address and, optionally, a link for donating. Tapping either hands off to your Mail app or your browser; Seek sends nothing itself. If you write to us, we see the address and message you send, and we use them only to reply. The donation page is run by a third party (Venmo) under its own privacy policy.

TestFlight feedback

If you test a pre-release build through Apple's TestFlight, any feedback or crash report you choose to send through TestFlight goes to us through Apple, under Apple's terms. Seek does not collect it on its own.

Deleting your data

Deleting Seek from your device deletes the study data stored in it, including the automatic copies in Seek's own folder. Backups and exports you saved elsewhere, and automatic copies in a folder you chose, are not deleted. You can also erase your reading history inside the app, as described above.

Children

Seek is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, because we do not collect information at all.

Changes to this page

If Seek ever changes how it handles information, for example by adding sync or a feature that uses the internet, we will update this page before the change reaches you, and say so in the app's release notes.
